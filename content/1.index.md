### We are a small, Australian and New Zealand-based, IT company specialising in Knowledge Graphs, Data Modelling &amp; Cataloguing and AI.

We use deep experience with formal spatial, relational and [Semantic Web](https://www.w3.org/standards/semanticweb/) data modelling methods, as well as multiple new AI methods, to build [Knowledge Graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_graph). With these we can make highly integrated data, do reasoning &amp; analytics and make high-quality feed-ins to several forms of AI.

Our [people](/about-us/our-people) are data modellers, cloud operators, AI engineers, vocabulary managers, cataloguing and data governance specialists with decades of research, government and private sector experience.

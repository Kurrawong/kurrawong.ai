# Services

The [People](/about-us/our-people) at KurrawongAI have a variety of skills to assist organisations across varied domains and aspects of Knowledge Graph, AI data and Data Governance applications.

Our services offered are:

- **Artificial Intelligence**
    - [System Building](/products/bower) - primarily through the deployment of our [Bower AI Framework Product](/products/bower), but not only, we can build AI systems
    - [Appropriateness Consulting](/services/ai-appropriateness) - we have lots of experience with many different forms of AI and can help you make sensible choices for its use
    - [Data Management](/services/ai-powered-data-management) - we use AI _a lot_ for Data Management and can help you with this too, with or without building an AI system for you
- **Knowledge Graphs**
    - [System Design & Implementation](/services/system-design) - architecture design, system design, ETL & data migration, implementation and customised branding views and APIs
    - [Maintenance & Support](/services/support) - one-off solutions or ongoing Service Level Agreements
    - [Related software tool services](/services/tools) - development, customisation or provisioning of tools for your organisation
    - [Complete Vocabulary Support](/services/vocabularies) - establishment, understanding and management
- **Data**
    - [Governance](/services/data-governance) - policies, reviews and training
    - [Modelling](/services/data-modelling) - domain-specific and enterprise data modelling, predicated on and Linked Data / Semantic Web / Knowledge Graph principles
    - [Reference Data Use](services/reference-data-use) - we use a lot of reference data within Knowledge Graphs and for AI context. We can help you establish Referecne Data systems and practices
    - [Tool Building](https://tools.kurrawong.ai) - we maintain a range of tools we and our clients use. We can make specialised data tools for you too
- [**Support**](/services/support) - we can support anything we build and even some things that we didn't build if they are in our general area
- [**Training**](/services/training) - across multiple aspects of Knowledge Graph data, formats and skill levels
